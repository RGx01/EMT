🩺 The health strip

Along the top, your data at a glance — each figure a link:

  • Import bill and Export bill — how many half-hours need a look, or all clear (with Octopus connected).
  • Missing — half-hours with no record at all, and in how many gaps.
  • Last backup — how long ago, and what made it.
  • Database — its size.

While a job is rewriting history (an import, a gap fill, a re-fetch) the counts wait until it finishes.

🔥 The data heatmap

The same month-by-month heatmap as on Charts, with views of the data's state as well as its energy:

  • Import bill — is each half-hour's cost final? Green: final, Octopus's cost (or, on a fixed-rate tariff, the settled kWh × its rate). Light amber: the kWh is settled, Octopus's cost not in yet. Amber: the kWh is not settled yet. Red: not final after 7 days, or something to look at. Teal: Octopus's cost with EMT's own car and house split. Blue: your correction. Grey: nothing to settle (imported history, or not on an Octopus agreement).
  • Export bill — the same for export, which is final once its kWh settles.
  • Missing — without Octopus, the one data view: is every half-hour recorded?

A missing half-hour is black and hatched on every view. The line under the heatmap's title says what each colour means. Issues only folds away the days with nothing to look at; a fold opens with a click.

🛠 The tools on a selection

Click a half-hour for its details. Drag across the heatmap (or click one cell and shift-click another) to select a span, then right-click it (a long press on a phone) for what can be done to it:

  • Re-fetch from Octopus — ask Octopus again for the span's kWh and cost, see what would change, and apply it or not.
  • Fill this gap — where the span holds missing half-hours: from Octopus, from a CSV (EMT gives you a template for the gap), or from your Octopus PDF bills.
  • Fill the missing export from PDF bills or a CSV.
  • Delete — the whole half-hours, import, export and the devices with them. Home Assistant's own readings are kept, so when the half-hours are filled again the devices are rebuilt from them.
  • Make a support file for the span.
Every change that cannot be undone takes a backup first.
🗂️ Whole history

The jobs for your whole history, in the menu at the top:

  • Import history from Octopus — the half-hours Octopus holds, about two years back.
  • Import a CSV and Import PDF bills — for history from before that, or from another supplier.
  • Recover every gap — ask Octopus for every missing half-hour it can fill.
  • Delete all imported history — the half-hours brought in by an import, after showing how many.

For a range, select it on the heatmap instead.

🗄️ Backup & restore
  • Backup now makes a backup at once; Backup & restore lists them — what made each, when, and its size — with the daily copy.
  • Restore one from the list, or drop a backup (a .zip or a blocks.db) anywhere on the page. EMT checks it before anything is touched, and a backup from the previous version of EMT is brought across.
  • Compact now rebuilds the database file to reclaim the space deleted rows left.
🩺 A support file

One zip to send to whoever is helping you: EMT's log, a summary of your setup and the machine it runs on, and your data for the span you choose. Hide my meter and account details (on by default) turns your MPANs, meter serials, account number and postcode into placeholders. Your Octopus API key is never in it, and nothing is sent anywhere — you download it.

⚡ Live power and the devices
  • Live Power — your grid power now: importing, exporting or net zero. It needs a power sensor for the main meter, or an Octopus Home Mini.
  • A gauge for each device with a power sensor: a battery's charge and its inverter (charging or discharging), an EV charger's draw, a heat pump's.
  • 🌿 Carbon Intensity — the grid's carbon now in your region, and its forecast for the next 48 hours. It needs a postcode.
  • 48-Hour Power History — your grid power over the last two days (kW); with a postcode, its carbon (CO₂) and the grid's fuel mix (Mix).
💷 Today, this bill and this year

Three cards — Today, This Bill (your current billing period) and This Year — each with its net cost so far, and the lines behind it: the total import, the house's direct import and each device beneath it, the grid export and the standing charge.

🚗 Smart Charging

On Intelligent Octopus Go, the card shows your car's last smart charge:

  • the day and the window it ran, its kWh and what it cost — est. until Octopus's bill for those half-hours arrives;
  • on the capped tariff, how much of the day's 6 hours of smart charging it used (Octopus counts the 6 hours from noon to noon). It reads ≈: it is EMT's estimate from the energy and the car's charging power, as Octopus does not say;
  • whether any of it was at the peak rate, and why — a bump charge or over the 6 hours;
  • any charging outside a smart charge, in the charge's noon-to-noon day;
  • the next charge Octopus has planned.

Expand the card for the last 7, 30 or 90 days of charges, grouped by noon-to-noon day. Octopus only gives recent dispatches, so for nights before EMT started recording them the charges come from Octopus's split of your bill, shown by the half-hour.

⚡ Agile prices

On Agile, your half-hourly prices from 24 hours ago to as far ahead as Octopus has published, with a card each for import and, if you export, export. Import is coloured cheap to dear (blue below 0p), export from blue to purple as it pays more, and now is a dashed line. Above them, the price now and the cheapest hour still to come for import, or the best-paid for export. Tomorrow's prices are published at about 4pm and run to 23:00, where Agile's day ends.

Not on Agile? With Octopus connected, the cards show Agile in your region — Agile's prices where you live, and Agile Outgoing's if you export — with what your last 30 days would have cost at them, against what you paid or earned (unit rates only; standing charges aside). EMT asks Octopus for them once a day, after 4pm.

Not interested? The eye on a card hides it, for everyone using this EMT, and EMT stops asking Octopus for those prices. To bring it back, use the link at the foot of the Overview.

👁 Hiding a card

The eye on a card hides it, for everyone using this EMT. Links at the foot of the page bring it back. Hiding only changes what the page shows: EMT keeps recording. The one exception is the Agile cards, where EMT also stops fetching the prices.

↕ Arranging the page

The cards sit in rows: Power (Live Power and your devices), Grid and carbon (the generation mix, carbon intensity and the 48-hour history), Smart Charging, the Agile import and export prices, and the costs. Unlock the padlock at the top right, then drag a row by the strip above it, or a card along its row by its ⠿ drag handle; lock it again when you are done. The order is kept for everyone using this EMT.

The Power row shares its width evenly: when its cards do not fit on one line, they split as evenly as they can, three and two rather than four and one. A hidden card's neighbours fill its place. The compact button on a power or cost card folds its whole row: the power cards to their gauges, the cost cards to a total each.

⚡ Power
  • 🏠 Meter — your grid power now: importing, exporting or net zero. It needs a power sensor for the main meter, or an Octopus Home Mini.
  • A gauge for each device with a power sensor: a battery's charge and its inverter (charging or discharging), an EV charger's draw, a heat pump's. On Intelligent Octopus Go's capped agreement (IOG-SMB) the car's card also shows today's 6 hours, noon to noon: about how much smart charging is used, how much tonight's plan adds, and what is left or over. A heat pump given an outdoor temperature sensor in Meter Config shows the temperature too, beside its power and dashed on its chart, on a scale of its own.

Beneath each gauge, today against yesterday: the energy since midnight, today's line over yesterday's (dimmed), with today's figure and yesterday's by the same time. Live Power shows your import and export, from the meter's half-hours. A device shows what its power sensor measured — what it used, from any source, which is not the share of the bill Billing gives it — and a battery or a V2X car what went out too, below the line. A battery with a charge sensor switches between its charge (SoC) and its energy (kWh); EMT remembers which. Point at a chart, or tap it, for the figures at that time. The expand button opens a card large, with its times and scale.

🌐 Grid and carbon
  • 🌐 Current Grid Generation Mix — what the grid is burning and spinning now. It needs a postcode.
  • 🌿 Carbon Intensity — the grid's carbon now in your region, and its forecast for the next 48 hours. It needs a postcode.
  • 48-Hour Power History — your grid power over the last two days (kW); with a postcode, its carbon (CO₂) and the grid's fuel mix (Mix).
💷 Today, this bill and this year

Three cards — Today, This Bill (your current billing period) and This Year — each with its net cost so far, and the lines behind it: the total import, the house's direct import and each device beneath it, the grid export and the standing charge.

With Octopus connected, the ⏳ pill at the top right says how many half-hours are still waiting for Octopus, as on Charts: a click opens Data Management on them, a right-click (a long press on a phone) retries settlement. See Charts' help for its stages.

🚗 Smart Charging

On Intelligent Octopus Go, the card shows your car's last smart charge:

  • the day and the window it ran, its kWh and what it cost — est. until Octopus's bill for those half-hours arrives;
  • on the capped tariff, how much of the day's 6 hours of smart charging it used (Octopus counts the 6 hours from noon to noon). It reads ≈: it is EMT's estimate from the energy and the car's charging power, as Octopus does not say;
  • whether any of it was at the peak rate, and why — a bump charge or over the 6 hours;
  • any charging outside a smart charge, in the charge's noon-to-noon day;
  • the next charge Octopus has planned.

Expand the card for the last 7, 30 or 90 days of charges, grouped by noon-to-noon day. Octopus only gives recent dispatches, so for nights before EMT started recording them the charges come from Octopus's split of your bill, shown by the half-hour.

⚡ Agile prices

On Agile, your half-hourly prices from 24 hours ago to as far ahead as Octopus has published, with a card each for import and, if you export, export. Import is coloured cheap to dear (blue below 0p), export from blue to purple as it pays more, and now is a dashed line. Above them, the price now and the cheapest hour still to come for import, or the best-paid for export. Tomorrow's prices are published at about 4pm and run to 23:00, where Agile's day ends.

With a power sensor, each card also draws your power over the last 24 hours as a line on its own kW scale, on the right: what you imported on the import card, what you exported on the export card (the 48-Hour Power card's readings, averaged five minutes at a time). Hover a half-hour for its price and its average power.

Not on Agile? With Octopus connected, the cards show Agile in your region — Agile's prices where you live, and Agile Outgoing's if you export — with what your last 30 days would have cost at them, against what you paid or earned (unit rates only; standing charges aside). EMT asks Octopus for them once a day, after 4pm.

Not interested? The eye on a card hides it, for everyone using this EMT, and EMT stops asking Octopus for those prices. To bring it back, use the link at the foot of the Overview.

⚙️ Meter Config

A tab each: Site, Main meter, one for each device, and + Add device. An edit on one tab survives moving to another, and Save Config saves them all; the engine picks the change up within seconds.

  • ↺ Refresh Entities — read Home Assistant's sensors again, after adding one.
  • ⚙ Change Setup — the setup wizard again: your supplier, your meter, where your readings come from.
  • 🕓 Billing History — your billing day, address and supplier changes over time.
🏠 Site and ⚡ Main meter
  • Site — its name (used throughout the charts), the first day of your billing period, the timezone, and the postcode prefix for carbon.
  • Main meter — the import and export read sensors (cumulative kWh from and to the grid), the rate and standing charge sensors, or Octopus's, and a power sensor for the live gauge. Each channel's Advanced section holds its MPAN and tariff, shown on the bill card, and its source.
🔋 Devices

A device is a load EMT splits out of your import: a battery, an EV charger, a heat pump or another.

  • Its read sensor — cumulative kWh, a total that only goes up. A charger or a heat pump reports the energy it used. A battery must report the energy it took from the grid, not all it charged: EMT cannot tell its solar charging apart. If its sensor is caught counting solar, EMT says so at the top of every page and changes nothing.
  • Its power sensors — optional, for the Overview's gauges only: a charger's or a heat pump's power, a battery's state of charge and inverter, solar power. A power sensor can be inverted, or its unit set, where it reports the other way round.
  • History sensors — older sensors the device used before its current one. ▶ Rebuild history rebuilds the device's past from Home Assistant's recorder, with them. Importing your history from Octopus or a CSV does this for a device with no history yet, once the import is done — unless its sensor reads more than the house drew, or far less, which the card then says. The card lists the sensors its history came from. ↻ Replace history is for a sensor that turned out to be wrong: after a backup, it drops what the device's rebuilt history and any sensor it no longer uses recorded, and rebuilds it all from the sensors it uses now. Changing a device's sensor asks why — renamed, a new device, or the wrong sensor, which offers the replace.
  • Its colour and rate line on the charts.
  • Archive — stop recording it from a date, keeping its history (↩ Unretire brings it back). ✕ Remove deletes it and its history, after a backup.

🚗 EV — with Octopus connected and an EV that smart-charges but no EV device, your car still appears in the charts, from Octopus's bills and dispatches. Its tab sets its colour; an EV device added later takes it on.

🌿 Carbon
  • The postcode prefix, if not given at setup.
  • Carbon equivalence — what a petrol car's mile, and the like, is taken to emit, for comparisons on Insights.
  • Battery and heat pump assumptions — the battery's round-trip efficiency; the heat pump's seasonal COP, for the comparison with a gas boiler.
  • Export carbon offset — how the carbon your export saved is counted: at the grid's average intensity, by default.

🔭 Choosing a period

Insights reads a period at a time: a Month or a Year, with the arrows to move between them. Beside a month, vs Last Month or vs Same Month Last Year; beside a year, vs Last Year. ⬇ PDF saves the tab you are on.

🌿 Carbon

The carbon your electricity carried, from the grid's intensity in your region. It needs a postcode.

  • Carbon Summary — the grid carbon you imported and what your export offset, and whether your use leaned to the grid's cleaner or dirtier half-hours.
  • Solar Export Offset — the carbon your export displaced from the grid.
  • Direct Import — the house's own share, without the devices.
  • Battery Charging Behaviour — whether the battery's grid charging was timed to the grid's cleaner periods.
  • Heat Pump Carbon — its carbon, and the grid intensity above which a gas boiler would have been cleaner.
  • EV Charging Carbon — the carbon of the car's charging.
  • Net carbon per billing period — a bar a period; click one to view its month.

A card shows only when you have the device it is about.

💷 Usage
  • Cost & Earnings — the period's net cost: grid import, export earnings, the standing charge and the average import rate.
  • Rate Brackets — the period's grid import by the price it was charged, in 2.5p brackets (inc VAT); a half-hour billed at two rates counts in both. It shows when the import fell in more than one bracket, and links to the same view, month by month, on Usage Stats.
  • Grid Position — import, export and the house's direct import in kWh, and how many days you exported more than you imported.
  • Peak Demand Window — the 2-hour window with the house's highest grid import.
  • A card for each device — its kWh and cost for the period.

📊 Billing

Your bill, a billing period at a time — or a month, quarter or year — with the arrows to move between them. The bill card sets out what the period cost, line by line, as a bill does.

  • vs Prev and vs Last Year set the period beside the one before, or the same one a year ago.
  • Censor blurs the meter number, the readings and the site's name, for a screenshot.
  • Show Data shows or hides every day's data table.
  • Each day has a chart: its half-hours' kWh as bars, a device each, and the rate as a line.
  • Correcting a rate — without the supplier API, right-click a day's chart (a long press on a phone) to correct a half-hour's rate or the day's standing charge. With Octopus connected the bill is Octopus's, so there is nothing to correct.
🔥 Heatmaps

A month at a time, a row a day and a cell a half-hour, coloured by its kWh, its carbon (gCO₂) or the grid's carbon intensity (gCO₂/kWh), with each day's total beside it. The data's health — what is settled and what is missing — is the same heatmap on Data Management.

📈 Usage Stats

Four views, chosen under Show:

Devices

  • A bar each period — HH (a day's half-hours), Daily, Monthly, Yearly — split into the house and each device, with export below the axis.
  • Span shows a stretch you pick: a chapter of your home (from one era's start to the next era's, of any kind, the latest to today, and Before the first era); an era on its own, from its start to its own end or today; or a tariff, from its start to its end, with each stretch between its price changes under it. A bar a day for a span of up to two months, a bar a month for a longer one.
  • Billing or Calendar periods; kWh, Cost or CO₂; the years shown, below.
  • Net draws import less export as a line; Inc. standing charge adds it to the cost.
  • Tariffs lays your Octopus tariffs along the bars (import above, export below); hover one for its prices. Eras marks your home's changes (below).
  • The table below follows the chart; drag the line between them to resize, and Copy to clipboard to take the table elsewhere.

Rate brackets

Your grid import by the price it was charged, in 2.5p brackets (inc VAT) — for the whole meter, the house, a device, or your export — so you can see how much of it fell on cheap or dear half-hours, on any tariff. % shows each bracket's share. Span works here as in Devices. Below the chart, the average price against the whole meter's, and a row a bracket.

Flow

  • Where your grid import went, as one chart for the span on screen: from the grid, through the price it was charged (Rate brackets' 2.5p bands; bands under 2% of the import together as Other), to the house and each device. Your export leaves the house through the price it earned, in the same 2.5p bands (under 2% together as Other export).
  • Daily a day, Monthly a month, Yearly the years ticked — or Span: an era's chapter or a tariff, as in Devices, drawn as one flow. (They are the bar views' HH, Daily and Monthly, named by what Flow totals.)
  • In kWh, Cost or CO₂. With Inc. standing charge, the standing charge is a flow of its own.
  • A negative price (an Agile plunge) is a band below 0p. In Cost, what it paid you flows from whatever used it to Plunge credit, and paying to export at a negative price flows from the house to Paid to export; the figures above the chart say what the net holds.
  • Hover a flow for its kWh, cost, price per kWh and carbon. The figures above the chart are the span's import, export and net; the table lists every flow.
  • EMT meters the grid side only: what your solar or battery supplied is not shown, so export can be larger than the house's grid import.

Running total

  • Your running totals side by side — Cost, Usage and, with carbon, Carbon — for Net, Import, Export, a device, or the standing charges.
  • Line or Spiral: along a time axis, or wound a year a turn.
  • Lifetime, from the first day; or Year by year, each year from nought, laid over each other.
  • The year starts on 1 January, your billing anniversary, a tariff's start or an era's — so the year before your solar and the year after it each start together.
  • The table: each year's total, its change on the year before, and its total to the same day of the year as the latest — like for like.
⏳ The settlement pill

With Octopus connected, the pill at the top says how many half-hours are still waiting for Octopus, at each stage: their kWh (the meter's settled reading), their bill (Octopus's cost), and, on the capped Intelligent tariff, their car and house split. Until then a half-hour is priced on EMT's own figures.

  • Click it for Data Management, with the half-hours that need a look.
  • Right-click it (a long press on a phone) to Retry settlement — ask Octopus now rather than at the next 6-hourly check (not again within 15 minutes).
⚙︎ Home eras and bar colours

The ⚙︎ beside PDF:

  • Home eras — a dated note for each change to your home: solar, a battery, a heat pump, an air-to-air unit, an EV, insulation, a house move, a meter change. EMT adds a house move and a meter change itself when it sees them in your Octopus account. Eras are marked on Usage Stats; they change no figure.
  • Bar colours — each meter's colour and rate line, in one place, as on each device's Settings tab.
⬇ PDF

Saves the tab you are on as a PDF, on a white page whatever your theme: the Billing tab with every day's chart, Usage Stats with its settings, chart and table.

📊 Billing

Your bill, a billing period at a time — or a month, quarter or year — with the arrows to move between them. The bill card sets out what the period cost, line by line, as a bill does.

  • vs Prev and vs Last Year set the period beside the one before, or the same one a year ago.
  • Censor blurs the meter number, the readings and the site's name, for a screenshot.
  • Show Data shows or hides every day's data table.
  • Each day has a chart: its half-hours' kWh as bars, a device each, and the rate as a line.
  • Correcting a rate — without the supplier API, right-click a day's chart (a long press on a phone) to correct a half-hour's rate or the day's standing charge. With Octopus connected the bill is Octopus's, so there is nothing to correct.
🔥 Heatmaps

A month at a time, a row a day and a cell a half-hour, coloured by its kWh, its carbon (gCO₂) or the grid's carbon intensity (gCO₂/kWh), with each day's total beside it. The data's health — what is settled and what is missing — is the same heatmap on Data Management.

📈 Usage Stats

Four views, chosen under Show:

Devices

  • Bars split into the house and each device, with export below the axis.
  • Period or Span chooses what is on screen, and HH, Day, Month or Year what each bar holds.
  • Under Period: HH shows a day's half-hours, Day a month's days, Month the months of the years ticked, Year the years; the row below picks the day, the month or the years.
  • Span shows a stretch you pick: a tariff, from its start to its end, with each stretch between its price changes under it; or an era, from its start to its own end or today, listed by kind as in the eras menu. Its bars are a Day, a Month or a Year (no HH on a span); until you choose, a bar a day for a span of up to two months, a bar a month for a longer one.
  • By the Month, and on a span by the day or the month, Date puts the bars in date order and Month groups them by month across the years (every January together, each year a shade darker). A bold line separates the months — twelve divisions — and by the day a fainter one each month's years; the Net line runs within each, not on into the next. Tariffs and eras show in date order only.
  • Billing or Calendar periods; kWh, Cost or CO₂; the years shown, below.
  • Net draws import less export as a line (on HH, the day's net so far); Running total, by the day, the month or the year, the net so far from the first bar, on its own axis — its last point is the table's Total. Not in month order. Inc. standing charge adds it to the cost.
  • Tariffs lays your Octopus tariffs along the bars (import above, export below); hover one for its prices. Eras marks your home's changes (below).
  • Zoom: the bar in the table's heading is a window on the bars — drag either end to zoom from that side, the middle to move along; ↺ (or a double-click on it) shows them all again. The chart, its lines and the table follow it. Not on HH.
  • The table below follows the chart; drag the line between them to resize, and Copy to clipboard to take the table elsewhere.

Rate brackets

Your grid import by the price it was charged, in 2.5p brackets (inc VAT) — for the whole meter, the house, a device, or your export — so you can see how much of it fell on cheap or dear half-hours, on any tariff. With more than a dozen brackets, as on Agile, the legend is a single colour scale: hover a colour to name its bracket and pick it out in the bars. % shows each bracket's share. Span works here as in Devices. Import & export, where you export, shows both: import above the axis and export below in kWh, each in its own colours (export's indigo to violet, and magenta where you paid to export). In Cost the bars are money: above the axis what you paid — import, or export at a negative price — and below what you were paid — export, or import in a plunge. Below the chart, the average price against the whole meter's, and a row a bracket.

Flow

  • Where your grid import went, as one chart for the span on screen: from the grid to the house and each device, and your export out of the house. The Grid and the Export bar are named with their totals in the measure shown. The house is as tall as its own import; its export starts at that size and widens (or narrows) to the export's.
  • Under Period, Day a day, Month a month, Year the years ticked — or Span: a tariff or an era, as in Devices, drawn as one flow.
  • In Cost, the import goes through the price it was charged (Rate brackets' 2.5p bands; bands under 2% of the cost together as Other), and the export leaves through the price it earned (under 2% together as Other export), the bands coming back together in one Export bar. With Inc. standing charge, the standing charge is a flow of its own, through each daily rate it was charged at (Standing 50.46p/day; hover one for its days). In kWh and CO₂ there are no price bands: the grid goes straight to where the energy went, and the export goes straight to its bar. In CO₂ the export flows out of the house, green, to Displaced by export: the grid carbon it displaced (at the grid's average for each half-hour, or the figure in Settings → Export Carbon Offset), not carbon through your house.
  • A negative price (an Agile plunge) is a band below 0p. In Cost, what it paid you flows from whatever used it to Plunge credit (each device is named with its net cost, after that credit), and paying to export at a negative price flows from the house to Paid to export; the figures above the chart say what the net holds.
  • Hover a flow for its kWh, cost and carbon together, its average price per kWh and its grams of CO₂ per kWh. The figures above the chart are the span's import, export and net; the table lists every flow.
  • EMT meters the grid side only: what your solar or battery supplied is not shown, so export can be larger than the house's grid import.

Running total

  • Your running totals side by side — Cost, Usage and, with carbon, Carbon — for Net, Import, Export, a device, or the standing charges.
  • Line or Spiral: along a time axis, or wound a year a turn.
  • Lifetime, from the first day; or Year by year, each year from nought, laid over each other.
  • Tariffs on a line marks a dot where each tariff started and a smaller hollow one where its unit rate or standing charge changed, import and export, on the line at that day's total; Lifetime also lays the tariffs in a band above the line, with a tick at each price change. Hover a dot or the band for the prices from that day. A tariff whose prices move by the half-hour or the day (Agile, Tracker) shows its start only.
  • The year starts on 1 January, your billing anniversary, a tariff's start or an era's — so the year before your solar and the year after it each start together.
  • The table: each year's total, its change on the year before, and its total to the same day of the year as the latest — like for like.
⏳ The settlement pill

With Octopus connected, the pill at the top says how many half-hours are still waiting for Octopus, at each stage: their kWh (the meter's settled reading), their bill (Octopus's cost), and, on the capped Intelligent tariff, their car and house split. Until then a half-hour is priced on EMT's own figures.

  • Click it for Data Management, with the half-hours that need a look.
  • Right-click it (a long press on a phone) to Retry settlement — ask Octopus now rather than at the next 6-hourly check (not again within 15 minutes): the meter's half-hours, then the bill for any it has just settled.
⚙︎ Home eras and bar colours

The ⚙︎ beside PDF:

  • Home eras — a dated note for each change to your home: solar, a battery, a heat pump, an air-to-air unit, an EV, insulation, a house move, a meter change. EMT adds a house move and a meter change itself when it sees them in your Octopus account. Eras are marked on Usage Stats; they change no figure.
  • Bar colours — each meter's colour and rate line, in one place, as on each device's Settings tab.
⬇ PDF

Saves the tab you are on as a PDF, on a white page whatever your theme: the Billing tab with every day's chart, Usage Stats with its settings, chart and table.

API import
Fetches each half-hour's kWh with its exact billed cost and off-peak/peak label — dispatch-aware, IOG included, reaching your full history. Imported blocks are flagged and reversible (Delete… on the heatmap, only the imported half-hours).
How it works & timing
If a slot comes back without its cost (the supplier can drop it under load), the import quietly re-fetches and prices it from the real figure. It can take a while: the API is rate-limited and gets stricter the harder it's pushed, so the import runs in paced, resumable steps and eases off when the hourly allowance runs low — pausing and resuming on its own. Other apps on the same account share that allowance (the supplier's own app, the Home Assistant integration, any other API tool), so it may slow or pause while one of those is busy. Just leave it running. Afterwards a background pricing check gives the smart-charge (IOG) half-hours their exact off-peak price — rate-limited the same way, self-paced; the pricing-health panel below shows its progress.
Confirm your sites

Build a CSV from your Octopus bills
For history older than the API can reach, EMT can reconstruct it from your Octopus PDF bills. Select the bill PDFs on your computer — EMT reads them, builds an Import and Export CSV, and imports them. Intelligent Octopus half-hour pages are transcribed exactly; flat/older tariffs and export are reconstructed from the bill totals (flagged). Each bill is checked against its own printed totals, so a bad read is caught, not silently imported.
Tip: select bills for a single meter/MPAN at a time. If your selection spans a house move (a different MPAN) it's split out and flagged — import one MPAN at a time.
